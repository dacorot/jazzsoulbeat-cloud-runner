# Music editorial references

These tracks define the preferred musical direction for Jazz Soul Beat. They
are editorial references only; they do not constitute rights approval or
creative release approval.

- 029 — "Nouvelle Noel": positive seasonal-jazz reference.
- 030 — "Deck the Halls A": positive restrained seasonal reference.
- 033 — "Oh Holy Night": positive calm seasonal reference.
- 039 — "Silent Night": excellent calm seasonal reference.
- 040 — "Oh, Little Town of Bethlehem": excellent calm seasonal reference.

## Target profile

Prefer soft, elegant jazz/lounge with relaxed or medium tempo, warm piano,
vibes, brushed or light kit, bass, subtle seasonal instrumentation, and a
smooth late-night atmosphere. Prioritize tracks that support long-form
listening without aggressive funk, driving grooves, or excessive energy.
