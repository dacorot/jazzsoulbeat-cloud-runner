# Music library capacity plan

The channel must never build a long-form video from an incomplete or
unverified library. The library agent owns capacity planning; it never approves
rights automatically.

## Minimum targets

- One 2–3 hour video: at least 30–45 distinct approved tracks, depending on
  average track duration and crossfade policy. The assembly gate targets at
  least 120 minutes and never silently stretches a short-test library.
- Three weekly videos with healthy rotation: at least 60 approved tracks, or
  240–300 approved minutes, split across playlist identities.
- Twelve monthly videos with seasonal rotation: target 180+ approved tracks,
  or 24–30 hours of approved audio, with no forced repetition inside a video.
- Each track must have verified title, artist, source, license, attribution,
  composition/performance/recording evidence and commercial-use status.

## Agent behavior

The library-capacity agent reports approved minutes, missing minutes, unique
track count, repeated-track risk, playlist coverage and seasonal gaps. It blocks
assembly when capacity or rights evidence is insufficient and creates a bounded
discovery/review queue for the next PDCA cycle.
