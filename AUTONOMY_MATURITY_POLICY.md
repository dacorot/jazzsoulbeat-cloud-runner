# Jazz Soul Beat — Autonomy Maturity Policy

## Phase 1 — Calibration

The owner approves creative audio, visual direction and the first final video.
Agents perform discovery, rights checks, technical QA, metadata and private uploads.

## Phase 2 — Supervised autonomy

After repeated successful cycles with stable hashes, style checks, rights evidence
and zero unresolved QA failures, agents may approve internal creative iterations
that match the approved style profile. They still stop on novelty, ambiguity,
rights changes or failed checks.

## Phase 3 — Production autonomy

Agents may select approved music, generate visuals, assemble videos, write English
metadata, run QA, create private uploads and prepare a publication candidate.
They may iterate automatically under PDCA and keep a complete audit trail.

## Permanent human gate

The owner reviews the final publication candidate and authorizes public release.
This includes public YouTube videos, public live streams, deletion, rights
exceptions and any material change outside the approved style profile.

## Automatic stop conditions

Agents must stop and notify the owner when:

- rights evidence is incomplete or changes;
- an artifact hash changes after approval;
- the style profile is exceeded;
- a new artist, source, license or visual technique appears;
- technical QA fails;
- a live source is missing or unstable;
- publication metadata is incomplete.

Autonomy increases only after the orchestrator records successful PDCA cycles. No
agent may grant itself a higher autonomy phase.
